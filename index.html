ctdcast: processing and reports for shipboard CTD and LADCP data

ctdcast takes shipboard CTD data from the raw Sea-Bird files to a compiled, documented dataset and a set of self-contained HTML reports:

  • Processing — the raw .hex (with its .xmlcon) or .cnv is read to netCDF (stage 1), the surface soak and deck records are flagged (stage 2), and Sea-Bird’s processing steps, calibration and QC are applied (stage 3), with products such as 1 dbar averages made from the result. Every step and its parameters are recorded in the files. LADCP profiles are converted alongside.

  • profiles.nc — the casts compiled on a common pressure grid, with CF and CCHDO variable names, the sensor catalog and each cast’s processing record.

  • Reports — HTML pages that work fully offline at sea (every figure embedded, no external requests): a summary page and map, a page per cast, section and time-series pages for the groups of casts you define, an interactive map, the sensors used, every processing step by run of casts, and an inventory of each compiled file.

The pages are described in Report output, the files in Data files (netCDF), and the stages in ctdcast processing framework.
